Got questions?

We've Got
Answers.

The questions we hear the most — answered honestly.

Still have a question? Our team responds in under 2 hours on WhatsApp or email.

Is Cash on Delivery (COD) available? +
Yes! COD is available across India. A ₹40 handling fee applies to COD orders. Pay when your package arrives — no online payment needed. You can also pay via UPI, cards, or net banking if you prefer.
How long does delivery take? +
We ship within 1–2 business days of placing your order. Delivery typically takes 3–5 business days depending on your location. Metro cities usually receive orders faster. You'll get a tracking link via SMS/email once dispatched.
What's your return and exchange policy? +
7-day hassle-free returns from delivery date. If you receive a damaged or wrong item, we'll replace it at no cost. For size issues, we offer free exchanges within 7 days. Visit Returns & Refunds for the full policy.
How do I find the right size? +
Our sizing guide is on every product page. We size for Indian bodies — our Medium fits chest 38–40" and height 5'7"–5'10". If you're between sizes, size up for a relaxed fit. For oversized styles, go with your regular size.
Are the tees really organic cotton? +
Every single tee we sell is made from 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton. No blends, no shortcuts. We believe if you're going to wear something against your skin all day, it should be free from harmful chemicals.
Do graphics fade or crack after washing? +
Our graphics are printed with water-based reactive inks and heat-set at high temperature. They're tested to last 50+ washes without cracking. For best results: wash inside-out in cold water, avoid tumble drying.
